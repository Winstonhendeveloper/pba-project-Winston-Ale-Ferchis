#Topic Ideas:
We plan to analyse customer churn and predict purchase behaviour using transaction data to optimize retention strategies.

Another idea is to analyze the impact on Robotaxi's industry in San Francisco after Cruise suddenly exited the market. Focusing on the following questions:
1) Did the robotaxi crash rate per million miles in San Francisco fall in the year after Cruise's permits were suspended on October 24, 2023, compared with the year before?
2) Did Waymo's own crash rate per million miles change after its main competitor left the road?
3) Did the total number of reported robotaxi crashes fall, or did Waymo's expansion offset Cruise's exit?
